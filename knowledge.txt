The Webfala Digital Skills for All Initiative is a Nigerian non-profit organization established in 2018 to bridge the digital divide.

Mission: To equip rural youths, girls, women, and underprivileged children with digital literacy, ICT, and STEM education.

Reach: Has impacted over 32,000 people across 16 states through more than 320 workshops and community programs

Founded/led by Nafisat Bakare.

SafeHer: STEM and digital skills training tailored for survivors of sexual and gender-based violence (SGBV).

Keeping Female in STEM: Focuses on technical skills and mentorship for girls and women in science and technology.

Women in AI Fellowship / AI Career Accelerator: Trains women in artificial intelligence and modern tech careers.

SheGrows Digital Bootcamp: A digital literacy and business growth program for women entrepreneurs.

Code Club Program: Teaches coding, programming, and robotics to children and youth.

Digital Citizenship and Online Safety Program: Promotes safe internet habits, media literacy, and online protection.

GovTracker: Promotes government accountability and civic transparency using digital tools.

STEAM Funfest: An interactive learning festival celebrating science, technology, engineering, arts, and mathematics

The Fellowship runs for 9 months weeks and starts every April.

Fellows meet on Monday through Thursdays from 10am to 2pm.

To graduate, each fellow must complete a final project and present it on demo day.
